What NourishFlow Brings Together

NourishFlow is a portfolio product concept built around a simple nutrition flow: compare meal styles, estimate daily energy needs, explore meal inspiration, and preview a request without creating an account.

Simple to Explore

The experience stays focused on the food, the calculator, and the next useful action, with clear controls that work across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Respectful by Design

No fake urgency, forced popups, tracking, or hidden form submission. The demo is intentionally lightweight and transparent.
